A support assistant that answers in Saudi dialect — and knows when to hand over

Customers of a travel agency ask about fees, refunds, baggage, visas and Umrah packages in Saudi dialect, formal Arabic or English. The assistant answers only from the company's written policy, and code checks every answer before it is sent. Anything it cannot prove — complaints, compensation, a specific booking, a question the policy doesn't cover — goes to a person.

Saffar Travel is a fictional company invented for this project; its policies and prices are made up.

Polite redirect

How it works

  1. Find the policy. Each policy line is embedded in Arabic and English with a multilingual embedding model; the 5 closest sections are shown to the model, plus an index of all 14 so it can ask for one the search missed.
  2. Decide. One model call returns an action — answer, hand over, or redirect — with the reply and the sections it used.
  3. Check with code. An answer is sent only if it cites a section that was shown, and every number in it is in that section or follows from the customer's numbers (250 × 3 passengers). Otherwise it goes to a person.
  4. Hand over cleanly. Complaints, compensation, actions on a booking, emergencies, legal threats, exceptions and anything the policy doesn't cover go to a person with a one-line summary.

How the test was kept honest

  • The 245 test messages were written and committed (7ebd4eb) before any assistant code existed, and were not edited after the first run. Tuning used a separate 78-message dev set.
  • Marking is done by code: the right action, a cited section from the expected list, and every expected figure present in the reply. No model grades another model.
  • The marking is strict: a correct reply that writes a number in words ("two bags") counts as wrong. The misses below show where that happened.
  • Mix of the test: 165 policy questions, 68 that need a person, 12 off-topic; 126 Saudi dialect, 40 Formal Arabic, 79 English.

اعتذر ووجّه

كيف يعمل

  1. إيجاد السياسة: كل سطر في السياسة مُمثَّل بالعربي والإنجليزي بنموذج تمثيل متعدد اللغات؛ تُعرض أقرب 5 أقسام على النموذج، مع فهرس الأقسام الـ 14 ليطلب قسمًا فاته البحث.
  2. القرار: استدعاء واحد للنموذج يرجع إجراءً — يجيب أو يحوّل أو يعتذر — مع الرد والأقسام التي استخدمها.
  3. المراجعة بالكود: لا تُرسل الإجابة إلا إذا ذكرت قسمًا عُرض فعلًا، وكان كل رقم فيها موجودًا في القسم أو ناتجًا من أرقام العميل (250 × 3 ركاب). وإلا تذهب لموظف.
  4. تحويل منظّم: الشكاوى والتعويضات وطلبات تنفيذ على حجز والطوارئ والتهديدات القانونية والاستثناءات وكل ما لا تغطيه السياسة تذهب لموظف مع ملخص بسطر واحد.

كيف حافظنا على نزاهة الاختبار

  • كُتبت رسائل الاختبار الـ 245 وحُفظت (7ebd4eb) قبل كتابة أي كود للمساعد، ولم تُعدَّل بعد أول تشغيل. التحسين تم على مجموعة منفصلة من 78 رسالة.
